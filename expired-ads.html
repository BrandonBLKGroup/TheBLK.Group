📥 How to Download & Use

  1. Click "VIEW AD →" on any creative above
  2. The ad will open in a new tab at exactly 1080×1080 pixels
  3. Take a screenshot:
    • Mac: Cmd + Shift + 4 then drag to select the ad
    • Windows: Win + Shift + S then drag to select the ad
  4. Or right-click the ad and select "Save Image As..."
  5. Upload to Meta Ads Manager (Feed placement: 1080×1080)

🎨 Need Customization?

Replace "REPLACE WITH HOME PHOTO" text with an actual Central Arkansas home photo for the background. Use a high-quality image of a nice home (preferably one that sold quickly with your team). The dark overlay will keep text readable.

🎯 Recommended Testing Strategy

Run all 3 creatives against your 1,100-lead audience simultaneously with equal budget. After 3-5 days, allocate more budget to the winner. Creative A typically performs best for expired listings (pain/pattern interrupt), but always test.
